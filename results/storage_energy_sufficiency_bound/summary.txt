Q6. Does this support the theory that storage-aware MC must preserve energy for scarcity hours?
  YES — the sufficiency bound shows that storage energy budget and its
  pre-positioning relative to shortage events is the primary determinant
  of EUE.  Models that charge from surplus (M1c, M1d, M2) naturally
  pre-position energy before events and achieve the bound.  Models that
  proactively discharge in non-shortage hours (M1, M1b) deplete SOC
  before shortage events, moving away from the bound — explaining their
  LOLH overestimation bias.  The bound is the theoretical justification
  for the M1c design principle: discharge only at shortfall hours.
