=======================================================================
ROOT CAUSE: Ramp constraints active in HOPE-ED, absent in M3
=======================================================================
Reading both model formulations directly revealed the mismatch:

  M3  (src/models/M3EDDispatch.jl):
      NO ramp constraints.  Only: 0 ≤ p[g,h] ≤ A[g,h] × Pmax[g]

  HOPE (HOPE_project/src/PCM.jl, constraints PCM-C3.RU / PCM-C3.RD):
      p[g,h] − p[g,h−1] ≤ RU_g × (1−FOR_g) × Pmax_g   (ramp-up)
      p[g,h−1] − p[g,h] ≤ RD_g × (1−FOR_g) × Pmax_g   (ramp-down)
      Applied to all thermal generators (G_F set).

The exporter script 25 was passing real M3 ramp rates (as RU/RD
fractions of Pmax per hour) into HOPE for BOTH ED and UC modes.

Mechanism — NGCC unit (Pmax = 355 MW, ramp = 4.14 MW/min):

    ru_frac = 4.14 × 60 / 355 = 0.6997
    HOPE ramp-down limit at hour h−1 = 0.6997 × 355 = 248.4 MW

    When unit transitions INTO forced outage at hour h:
      M3:       p[g, h−1] can reach 355.0 MW (no ramp constraint)
      HOPE-ED:  p[g, h−1] ≤ 248.4 MW (ramp-down constraint binding)
      Gap = 355.0 − 248.4 = 106.6 MW

    If hour h−1 is a shortage hour: M3 avoids 106.6 MWh of load
    shedding that HOPE-ED cannot avoid.

Per-scenario accounting (all exact matches):
  s002: 1 NGCC outage-transition × 106.6 MW × 1 h  → +106.60 MWh ✓
  s003: 1 NGCC outage-transition × 106.6 MW × 1 h  → +106.60 MWh ✓
  s004: 2 NGCC outage-transitions × 106.6 MW × 1 h → +213.20 MWh ✓
  s005: CoalSteam-350 (350 MW, ramp 6.83 MW/min):
        350 × (1 − 6.83×60/350) = 350 × (1−0.6857) = 110.0 MW
        1 outage-transition × 110.0 MW × 1 h        → +110.00 MWh ✓

All other parameter differences (storage efficiency, initial SOC,
VOLL, operating reserves, Pmin, outage state encoding) were verified
identical and ruled out as root causes.

=======================================================================
FIX (scripts/25_build_hope_full_year_cases.jl, lines 243–249)
=======================================================================
  BEFORE (wrong — same ramp fraction for both modes):
      ru[g] = ru_frac
      rd[g] = ru_frac

  AFTER (correct):
      # ED: RU=RD=1.0 disables effective ramp limits (max ramp = Pmax),
      #     matching M3's LP which has no ramp constraints.
      # UC: keep real ramp rates — meaningful operational constraint.
      ru[g] = is_ed ? 1.0 : ru_frac
      rd[g] = is_ed ? 1.0 : ru_frac
