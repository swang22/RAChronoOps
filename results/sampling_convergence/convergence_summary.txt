  Mean event duration:  M3 = 3.03 h, M1c = 3.03 h (Δ=+0.00 h), M2 = 2.07 h (Δ=-0.96 h)

  duration differences, not to n_events.

Q8. Should the paper include this convergence experiment in main text or appendix?
  Recommendation: Appendix.
  Rationale:
  - The N=20 common-random-number method comparison is the primary contribution.
    It shows method error is zero regardless of N.
  - Showing CI95 shrinkage quantifies how reliable the N=20 absolute estimates
    are — a useful robustness check, but secondary to the method comparison.
  - Event-shape metrics (duration, energy, shortfall) can go in an appendix
    table to support the paper's use of multiple reliability metrics.
  - If reviewers ask about statistical convergence, this experiment provides
    a complete answer.
