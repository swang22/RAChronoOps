Q2. Does M1d_largest reduce CVaR/p95 vs M1d_earliest?
  M1d_earliest:  CVaR = 9782.93 MWh,  p95 = 9719.41 MWh
  M1d_largest:   CVaR = 9782.93 MWh,  p95 = 9719.41 MWh
  NO difference at the scenario level — CVaR and p95 are identical.
  Explanation: scenario-level EUE is identical across modes because both
  allocate the same stored energy budget within each event (total discharge
  is conserved). The largest_first mode shifts load shed from smaller shortfall
  hours to larger ones WITHIN the event window, but the total unserved energy
  per scenario is unchanged. CVaR and p95 are computed from scenario-level EUE
  and therefore cannot differ. A within-event peak-shortfall analysis (e.g.,
  max_shortfall_mw per event) would reveal the mode difference.
  Observation: M1d_largest LOLH is higher than M1d_earliest (+2.4 h for
  VRE120_base). Largest_first leaves small shortfall hours partially covered
  (positive load shed) while fully covering the dominant event hours — spreading
  the energy across fewer but more severe shedding hours increases LOLH.
